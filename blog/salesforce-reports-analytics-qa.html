Salesforce Reports & Analytics Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on Salesforce documentation

Notes on turning Salesforce data into insight: standard reports and dashboards versus CRM Analytics, and what drives the choice between them.

1.When do standard Salesforce reports stop being sufficient, pushing a team toward CRM Analytics?

Standard reports handle straightforward filtering, grouping, and summarization well. CRM Analytics becomes valuable when a team needs to blend data from multiple sources, build predictive models, or interact with very large datasets beyond what report-builder performance comfortably supports.

2.What is the difference between a report and a dashboard?

A report is a detailed, filterable list or summary of records. A dashboard visualizes one or more reports as charts/metrics for at-a-glance monitoring — a dashboard is a presentation layer over underlying report data, not a replacement for the report itself.

3.Why does dashboard refresh frequency matter for how it's used operationally?

A dashboard that refreshes daily is fine for trend review but unsuitable for real-time operational monitoring (like live case queue volume), where a more frequent or on-demand refresh is needed to reflect current conditions accurately.

4.What consideration matters most when deciding report/dashboard sharing and folder access?

Reports can expose sensitive aggregated data even to users who can't see individual records directly, so folder-level sharing needs to be reviewed independently from object-level security — aggregation can leak information that row-level rules alone wouldn't.

Sources: Salesforce Help documentation (Reports & Dashboards, CRM Analytics). These are original notes and summaries, not reproduced source material.
