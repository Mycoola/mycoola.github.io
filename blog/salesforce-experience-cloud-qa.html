Salesforce Experience Cloud Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on Salesforce developer documentation

Notes on building external-facing sites and portals with Experience Cloud, including the newer LWR (Lightning Web Runtime) site type and identity considerations.

1.What is an LWR site, and how does it differ from a standard Aura-based Experience Cloud site?

LWR sites are built on Lightning Web Components for faster performance and simpler theming, aimed at public-facing sites where load speed matters more than deep Aura component compatibility that older Experience Cloud templates supported.

2.Why would a team choose an LWR site over an existing Aura-based template for a new build?

New builds generally favor LWR for its performance profile and alignment with LWC as the platform's primary component model, reserving Aura-based templates mainly for maintaining sites that already depend on components not yet available in LWR.

3.What role does Salesforce Identity play for an Experience Cloud site's external users?

Identity manages authentication, registration, and login for external community/portal users separately from internal employee licenses — letting an org expose a controlled slice of functionality to customers or partners without granting full internal access.

4.Why does branding an org in Lightning Experience matter beyond visual polish?

Consistent branding across internal and external-facing surfaces reinforces trust and reduces user confusion, especially when internal employees and external community users interact with related but differently-themed instances of the same org.

Sources: Salesforce Developers documentation (LWR Sites for Experience Cloud, Salesforce Identity). These are original notes and summaries, not reproduced source material.
