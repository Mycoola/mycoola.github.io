Salesforce Certification & Career Prep Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on certification guides and career articles

Notes on preparing for Salesforce certifications and building a career on the platform — from choosing a first certification to using Trailhead effectively.

1.Why is Trailhead recommended as a certification-prep foundation rather than exam dumps?

Trailhead's hands-on modules build the actual skills the exam tests — configuring real features in a Trailhead Playground — which produces durable, applicable knowledge rather than memorized answers that don't transfer to real work.

2.What makes the Platform App Builder certification a common early choice for career changers?

It focuses on declarative configuration (objects, layouts, automation) without requiring programming, making it accessible to people transitioning from non-technical roles while still being professionally credible on a resume.

3.Why is the JavaScript Developer I certification relevant to Salesforce careers even though it's not Salesforce-specific?

Lightning Web Components are built on standard JavaScript, so demonstrating JavaScript competency signals readiness for LWC development work, complementing Salesforce-specific certifications with a broader front-end skill credential.

4.What is the value of maintaining certifications through the release maintenance modules rather than treating certification as a one-time achievement?

Salesforce ships three releases a year, and features change. Maintenance modules confirm a certified professional's knowledge stays current, which protects both the credential's value and the professional's actual on-the-job competency.

5.Why do many Salesforce professionals build a public portfolio or blog alongside certifications?

Certifications validate baseline knowledge, but a portfolio demonstrates applied problem-solving on real (or realistic) scenarios — showing hiring managers evidence of judgment and communication skills that a multiple-choice exam can't capture.
