Salesforce Sales Cloud Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on Salesforce Trailhead and Help documentation

Notes on the core sales workflow in Salesforce: opportunities, leads, sales paths, and how accounts/contacts relate to each other in Lightning Experience.

1.What is the functional difference between a lead and a contact in the standard sales process?

A lead represents an unqualified prospect not yet tied to an existing account. Converting a lead creates (or links to) an account, contact, and optionally an opportunity — the conversion step is where an unproven prospect becomes a structured part of the account/contact data model.

2.What is a Sales Path meant to accomplish beyond the standard page layout?

A Sales Path highlights key fields and guidance specific to the current stage of a record (like an opportunity's stage), nudging reps toward the right next action instead of leaving them to interpret a static, stage-agnostic layout on their own.

3.Why does customer retention rate get tracked as a distinct metric from new revenue growth?

Retention measures how well existing relationships are preserved, which is typically cheaper to maintain than acquiring new customers — tracking it separately surfaces churn risk that a pure revenue-growth metric could mask if new sales offset losses.

4.What does an Account Intelligence or Contact Intelligence view add to standard account/contact pages?

These views surface engagement signals and health indicators (recent activity, sentiment, relationship strength) directly on the record, giving reps a faster read on relationship status without manually piecing it together from related lists.

Sources: Salesforce Trailhead (Accounts and Contacts, Sales Path modules) and Salesforce Help. These are original notes and summaries, not reproduced source material.
