Salesforce Architecture, Commerce & Marketing Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on Salesforce documentation

A combined set of notes on three smaller topic areas: Salesforce's Well-Architected design guidance, Commerce Cloud fundamentals, and Marketing Cloud/Pardot basics.

Architecture

1.What is the purpose of the Salesforce Well-Architected framework?

It codifies proven patterns and anti-patterns across trust, ease, and adaptability so architects have a shared vocabulary and checklist for evaluating design decisions, rather than each project reinventing architectural judgment from scratch.

2.Why does a design pattern need an explicit anti-pattern counterpart to be useful?

Naming the anti-pattern makes a bad design recognizable in code review or architecture discussions — a positive pattern description alone doesn't always make it obvious when a real implementation has quietly drifted into the problematic version.

Commerce

3.What is the core difference between B2B and B2C commerce configurations on the platform?

B2B commerce typically involves negotiated pricing, account-based buying, and approval workflows for bulk orders, while B2C is oriented around individual consumer self-service checkout — the underlying platform differs in how pricing, accounts, and order flow are modeled.

4.Why would a company evaluate Commerce Cloud instead of a general-purpose e-commerce platform?

Using Commerce Cloud on the same platform as CRM data keeps customer, order, and service data unified without needing a separate integration layer between a standalone e-commerce system and Salesforce — a tradeoff of platform lock-in for integration simplicity.

Marketing

5.What is the relationship between Marketing Cloud and the core Salesforce CRM?

Marketing Cloud can operate somewhat independently for campaign execution but delivers the most value when synced with CRM data (leads, contacts) so marketing engagement informs sales follow-up and vice versa, rather than the two systems working from disconnected audiences.

Sources: architect.salesforce.com Well-Architected guides, Salesforce Commerce and Marketing Cloud documentation. These are original notes and summaries, not reproduced source material.
