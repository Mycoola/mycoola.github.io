Tech & Science Concepts Q&A

August 2026  ·  Original notes based on English Wikipedia

Short reference notes on a handful of technical concepts encountered while browsing Wikipedia: what distinguishes AI from AI engineering, how software and data engineering relate, and what OPML and webhooks actually do.

1.What is the difference between artificial intelligence as a field and AI engineering as a discipline?

AI is the broad field studying how to make machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — reasoning, perception, language. AI engineering is the applied discipline of designing, building, and operating AI-based systems reliably in production, closer to software engineering than to research.

2.How does data engineering relate to software engineering?

Data engineering is a specialization within software engineering focused on building the pipelines, storage, and processing systems that move and transform data reliably, so that other systems (including AI models) can consume trustworthy data at scale.

3.What problem does a webhook solve compared to polling an API?

Polling repeatedly asks "has anything changed yet?" A webhook flips that: the source system pushes a notification the moment an event occurs, removing the need for the consumer to poll and reducing both latency and wasted requests.

4.What is OPML used for, and why does it matter for content syndication?

OPML (Outline Processor Markup Language) is an XML format for exchanging lists of outlines — most commonly used to export and import a collection of RSS/podcast feed subscriptions between different readers, without re-entering every feed manually.
